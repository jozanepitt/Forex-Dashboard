TDI Cycle 123 (StrictlyCorrect / Inducement Cycles): This is the "improvements on old BTMM" scanner. Where classic BTMM relies on subjective Level 1/2/3 templates and often mistimes entries, this scanner adds three objective filters: (1) FSO_TDI extreme — baseline (RSI SMA-34, "yellow line") reaches ≤ 37 or ≥ 63 when point 3 forms; (2) 123 Peak Formation geometry — point 3 must retest point 1 within ±25 % of the 1→2 leg; (3) Regular divergence 1→3 — price reaches an equal-or-more-extreme level at point 3 while the TDI RSI reaches a less-extreme one (bearish: price higher high + TDI lower high; bullish: price lower low + TDI higher low). Scored, not mandatory: ✓ regular = price pushed past point 1 (3 pts), ✓ equal-level = equal extreme within 5 % of leg 1 (2 pts), ✗ none = price fell short of point 1, so price and TDI are agreeing — a shallow-miss retest, which is valid geometry but earns no divergence points. ⚠ vs trend = real divergence, but fired against a fully-stacked EMA cascade (1 pt only). Per Davit's Pivot Trading with TDI p.9: "when the market is in a strong trend in either direction, oscillators do not function well … any signs of divergence during a strong trend would be ambiguous at best." (4) Weekly-pivot Location — Fibonacci pivots (R/S 38/61/78/100/138) projected from the prior week's range. A sell wants resistance (R-zones), a buy wants support (S-zones): ★ R/S 100, ✓ R/S 61–78, ~ R/S 38, ✗ mid-pivot / ✗ wrong side = low probability. Note: a 30-day backtest found the 61–100 zone did not separate winners from losers on this data, so location is shown for context but is not scored as an edge — the only hard rule is that a wrong-side setup (selling into support / buying into resistance) is capped to Grade C. Targets stay on the BTMM cascade: L1 = 50 EMA, L2 = 200 EMA, L3 = 800 EMA — when an EMA sits on the wrong side of price it's skipped, and if all three are invalid the row falls back to 1×/2×/3× measured-move projections (flagged "≈ measured move" in the table, never shown as if they were real EMA hits).
